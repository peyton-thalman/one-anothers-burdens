What’s inside

Eight parts to every chapter

Each chapter keeps one of the original guide’s twelve principles and its scripture, then rebuilds everything around it for any kind of affliction.

  1. 01The big ideaThe core of the principle in plain words.
  2. 02What the scriptures teachKey verses, with a line on why they matter here.
  3. 03From prophets and apostlesQuotes from general conference, each linked to the full talk.
  4. 04In real lifeHow the principle looks across illness, disability, mental illness, addiction, grief, and more.
  5. 05Also worth knowingWhat caregivers, counselors, and researchers have learned.
  6. 06Questions worth sitting withQuestions to ponder, journal, or discuss.
  7. 07Practice cardThe handout. One main practice for the week, plus a few options.
  8. 08Go deeperTalks, scriptures, and books.
